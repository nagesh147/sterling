# Markets

Sterling supports Indian cash and derivatives markets through Zerodha Kite and TrueData.

Supported product families:

- NSE/BSE equities
- NSE index and stock futures
- NSE index and stock options
- Indian volatility and breadth inputs used by the strategy engines

Prices and P&L are denominated in INR. Quantity validation follows exchange lot sizes, tick sizes, freeze limits, and broker product rules.
