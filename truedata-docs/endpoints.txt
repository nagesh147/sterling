Overview
This document outlines the Market Data APIs and their respective functionalities. These APIs
provide data on Historical data, Index, futures and options contracts, open interest (OI)
movements, option chain details, and market movers. They help identify OI gainers/losers,
most active securities, and top performing or underperforming indices and industries. The
following tables provides a summary of the available APIs along with a brief description of
their purpose.
